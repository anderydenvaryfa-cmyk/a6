Cookie & Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-ELAS

1. Educational Policy Statement and Purpose

This Cookie and Digital Telemetry Policy clarifies how Elastane Pair Atelier employs minimal, privacy-centric tracking technologies across elastanepair.com to deliver an elegant, uninterrupted digital salon experience. As a dedicated technical textile house, we fundamentally reject intrusive commercial behavioral profiling, cross-platform retargeting, and aggressive programmatic advertising trackers. Our digital infrastructure deploys cookies exclusively to sustain essential site navigation, maintain secure fitting reservation sessions, and record user accessibility selections throughout our online portfolio.

2. Essential Operational and Security Tokens

Essential cookies are small, secure cryptographic data strings placed on your local browsing terminal to allow seamless transitions between pages, prevent cross-site request forgery, and maintain active bespoke consultation form data. These technical mechanisms are strictly operational in nature; without them, dynamic features such as our interactive graduated compression calculator, appointment scheduling widgets, and protected contact portals cannot operate reliably. Essential session tokens expire automatically once you close your active browser session or complete form submission.

3. Aggregated Performance Telemetry and Analytics

To gauge which hosiery treatises, circular knit photomicrographs, and compression specifications generate the greatest interest among discerning athletes, our servers record anonymous, aggregated browsing telemetry. These metrics evaluate page render latencies, server response benchmarks, and general geographic distribution without associating telemetry to personally identifiable patron records. Analytical data is reviewed solely by our internal design team to resolve technical bottlenecks, enhance mobile navigation, and optimize our digital monograph library.

4. Absolute Exclusion of Advertising Retargeting

Elastane Pair Atelier maintains a strict, permanent policy prohibiting third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, and commercial data-broker tracking beacons across all hosted URLs. We do not participate in commercial programmatic ad exchanges, behavioral monetization syndicates, or cross-device profile harvesting networks. Your exploration of our technical hosiery portfolio remains completely private and unmonitored by commercial marketing conglomerates, ensuring an uncompromised environment dedicated purely to craftsmanship and textile excellence.

5. Client Configuration and Browser Controls

Patrons and web visitors maintain complete statutory autonomy to configure their individual browser environments to accept, block, or delete technical cookies at their discretion. Most modern desktop and mobile browsers provide dedicated privacy settings allowing users to clear browsing caches, restrict third-party scripts, or navigate in private browsing windows. Please be advised that disabling essential technical cookies may prevent proper functionality of our interactive compression gauge calculator and online studio booking tools.

6. Institutional Telemetry Registry and Contact

If you require further regulatory clarification regarding our digital telemetry practices, wish to inspect our technical cookie registry, or need assistance managing privacy configurations, please contact our information security team. Inquiries may be directed by mail to Elastane Pair Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by telephoning our administrative office at +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours for prompt technical assistance.
