Textile Heritage & Engineering Charter

Reinventing Hosiery Through Polymer Physics

For generations, the sock was relegated to an afterthought: a disposable tube of low-gauge cotton prone to sagging, bunching, and premature heel wear. At 181 Mercer Street, Elastane Pair was founded to elevate hosiery into a precision biomechanical instrument.

1. The Molecular Miracle of Segmented Polyurethane

At the heart of our technical socks lies elastane, a synthetic linear block copolymer developed through advanced polymer chemistry. By synthesizing alternating soft, amorphous polyether segments that provide up to six hundred percent reversible elongation with hard, rigid polyurethane segments that provide hydrogen-bonded physical crosslinks, elastane delivers instantaneous elastic snap-back that no natural fiber can match. When core-spun with ultra-fine Merino wool, it yields a textile that breathes, regulates micro-climate temperatures, and holds its structural memory across hundreds of wash cycles.

2. Italian 200-Needle Circular Cylinder Architecture

In our knitting suites, we utilize electronic Lonati circular cylinders equipped with two hundred ultra-fine needles operating in a tiny three-and-three-quarter-inch diameter cylinder. This high needle count produces a dense, compact stitch weave that shields the foot from abrasive friction while allowing micro-fine air channels to vent heat and humidity away from the epidermis.
