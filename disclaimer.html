Biomechanical & Technical Disclaimer

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-ELAS

1. Biomechanical Information and Educational Scope

The technical monographs, polymer tensile calculations, circular knitting analyses, and graduated compression tables published across elastanepair.com are compiled exclusively for educational, athletic, and textile reference objectives. While all denier ratings, mmHg compression numbers, and elastic hysteresis data are compiled with rigorous scientific fidelity, content on this domain does not constitute professional vascular surgical advice or formal medical prescriptions. Individuals suffering from severe peripheral arterial disease or advanced venous thrombosis should consult a licensed physician before utilizing high-compression garments.

2. Athletic Performance and Physical Wear Cautions

While Elastane Pair hosiery is engineered to optimize venous blood flow, reduce muscular vibration, and prevent friction hot-spots, individual athletic performance depends on multifaceted physiological variables. Wearing technical compression socks does not replace proper cardiovascular training, adequate hydration, or balanced athletic nutrition during endurance competitions. Elastane Pair Atelier disclaims liability for personal athletic injuries, muscular strains, or dermatological conditions resulting from improper sock sizing, neglected hygiene, or overuse in uncalibrated athletic environments.

3. Photographic Depiction and Screen Color Nuances

We photograph all finished hosiery models, knitted ribbed textures, and yarn swatches under color-calibrated fifty-five hundred kelvin daylight studio illumination to represent true fabric tones accurately. However, because individual computer monitors, mobile screens, and display panels utilize differing color gamuts, ambient brightness settings, and contrast curves, physical fabric hues may vary slightly from digital representations. We invite prospective patrons to visit our 181 Mercer Street studio to inspect physical knit swatches and experience our yarn textures in person.

4. Bespoke Lead Times and Precision Timelines

Stated delivery milestones for custom corporate orders and personalized team knitting represent conscientious professional estimates based upon current circular machine capacity and seasonal yarn availability. Unforeseen delays arising from specialized Italian cylinder re-tooling, custom yarn package dyeing, or cleanroom laboratory testing may occasionally necessitate extended production intervals. Elastane Pair Atelier disclaims liability for secondary scheduling inconveniences resulting from minor craftsmanship delays necessary to preserve our uncompromising standard of textile excellence.

5. External Textile Citations and References

Our technical treatises frequently cite international textile standards, academic biomechanics journals, and clinical vascular studies authored by independent research scholars. These scholarly references and hyperlinks are provided solely for academic context, scientific enrichment, and patron convenience. Elastane Pair Atelier exercises no operational control over external third-party digital repositories and assumes no responsibility for the accuracy, uptime, or copyright compliance of materials hosted on third-party academic or institutional websites.
