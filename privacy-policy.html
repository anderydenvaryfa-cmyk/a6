Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-ELAS

1. Institutional Scope and Client Privacy Mandate

At Elastane Pair Atelier, accessible through elastanepair.com, safeguarding the absolute confidentiality, personal security, and transaction privacy of all patrons and research collaborators is our primary administrative covenant. We deploy comprehensive cryptographic protocols, secure firewall perimeters, and physical record safeguards to protect private identities, biomechanical gait scans, and customized fitting specifications against unauthorized access, loss, or unlawful distribution. Under no circumstances do we lease, monetize, or disclose client dossier records to third-party commercial marketing syndicates or behavioral data brokers across any jurisdiction worldwide.

2. Collection of Fitting Data and Digital Telemetry

When scheduling custom fitting appointments at our Mercer Street studio, ordering technical compression socks, or submitting corporate athletic team orders, we gather necessary identity information including full legal names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers, and email coordinates. This operational data enables our technical hosiery specialists to coordinate diagnostic consultations, calculate custom graduated compression profiles, and maintain accurate order provenance archives across our customer care registry. Furthermore, our web hosting servers automatically log standard network telemetry, including internet protocol addresses and browser types, to protect platform integrity.

3. Essential Session Cookies and Browsing Security

Our digital platform deploys strictly essential technical cookies designed to sustain secure session authentication, facilitate rapid page navigation, and preserve user accessibility configurations across our online hosiery archive. These functional cookies operate exclusively within our primary domain and never harvest cross-domain browsing histories, monitor third-party web visits, or assemble commercial marketing dossiers. Patrons may adjust their local browser settings to decline non-essential cookies at any time, though doing so may limit interactive features such as our digital compression calculator and online appointment scheduling tools.

4. Authorized Personnel Access and Non-Disclosure

Client fitting specifications, transaction ledgers, and gait diagnostic records are restricted exclusively to authorized atelier concierges, textile engineers, and secure database administrators bound by comprehensive non-disclosure agreements. We never disclose private patron portfolios to outside parties, except when strictly necessary to complete insured courier package shipments or fulfill mandatory statutory tax compliance directives under federal commercial laws. Any external parcel carriers or financial processors contracted by our atelier must demonstrably satisfy international information security benchmarks, including full end-to-end data encryption.

5. Secure Data Retention and Deletion Privileges

We preserve private transaction documentation, custom knitting specifications, and fitting consultation communications exclusively for durations required to support our hosiery quality guarantee and satisfy statutory commercial auditing rules. When statutory retention mandates expire, personal digital profiles are permanently purged from our redundant server infrastructure upon formal patron request. Registered clients maintain full statutory privileges to demand a certified extract of their stored records, correct inaccurate personal information, or request complete removal from non-essential promotional correspondence at any time.

6. Institutional Privacy Officer and Contact Desk

Patrons and web visitors seeking legal clarification regarding our privacy protocols, wishing to exercise statutory data privileges, or requiring administrative assistance may contact our privacy compliance desk directly. Formal correspondence may be addressed by post to Elastane Pair Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted electronically via our verified concierge email. Alternatively, patrons may telephone our direct office line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard atelier business hours for prompt personal assistance.
