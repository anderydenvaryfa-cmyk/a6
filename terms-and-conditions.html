2. Custom Fitting Protocols and Retainers

Every custom team hosiery commission and private studio diagnostic fitting requires formal written validation and a non-refundable initial retainer before circular machine programming begins. Because bespoke hosiery commissions entail specialized Italian needle cylinder setup, high-denier covered elastane allocation, and dedicated cleanroom quality inspection by textile technicians, custom orders cannot be cancelled once yarn warping commences. Clients receive milestone notifications and macro studio progress photographs as their commissioned socks advance through our 181 Mercer Street facility.

3. Craftsmanship Standards and Seam Integrity Warranty

We warrant all handcrafted technical socks against manufacturing defects, elastic delamination, and seam failure under normal athletic usage for twelve full months from delivery. Because our 200-needle circular knit incorporates double-covered elastane cores and hand-linked Rosso seamless toe closures, we guarantee blister-free anatomical alignment provided the socks are laundered according to our published cold-water care instructions. Damages resulting from sharp footwear friction tears, pet claws, or harsh bleach exposure are explicitly excluded from warranty indemnification.

4. Limitation of Manufacture Operational Liability

Elastane Pair Atelier, its textile engineers, and corporate trustees shall not be held liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from digital platform downtime, commercial postal delays, or international yarn supply disruptions. Our maximum aggregate legal financial liability relating to any commissioned hosiery order shall not exceed the verified transactional fees paid directly to our atelier by the patron. We maintain comprehensive commercial liability insurance and fully insured, tracked shipping protocols across all domestic and international shipments.

5. Handcrafted Natural Fiber Variations

Technical socks incorporating core-spun Australian Merino wool utilize natural animal protein fibers that display subtle tonal variations and organic texture nuances unique to each shearing clip. Patrons acknowledge that minor surface fiber loft differences, heathered shading variations, and natural yarn slub contours are authentic hallmarks of genuine high-grade textile materials rather than manufacturing flaws. These natural fiber characteristics enhance thermal breathability and moisture management without compromising elastane structural elasticity.
